Corrections

Send the error before you send the complaint.

This product sells accuracy, so the correction channel opens before the first edition is sold. It stays open.

What counts

  • A wrong number. Bytes, counts, ratios that do not match the frozen files.
  • A wrong class. A site marked TRAP that in fact served markdown, or the reverse.
  • A method error. Something we did that invalidates the result — wrong header, wrong path, wrong denominator.
  • A missing limit. A way this edition could be read that we did not warn against.

What happens

Factual corrections are printed in the next edition, with the original left in place. The published file is not edited after the fact. If the error is material, the correction goes out on its own before the next edition.

Where to send it

Reply to the launch post or DM @afterhuman_ai. Say the site, the check, and what the response actually was.

Include the site, the check, and what the response actually was. Reproduction commands are published, so a disagreement should be settleable by rerunning them.
